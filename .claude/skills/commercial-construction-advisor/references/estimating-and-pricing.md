# Estimating, pricing, and risk

Source authorities: ASPE, AACE International, RSMeans, CII risk research, LCI Target Value Design.

## Estimating discipline

- [ ] Historical unit-cost database (by CSI division, sector, region)
- [ ] Conceptual / SD / DD / CD estimate accuracy bands (±25% / ±15% / ±10% / ±5%)
- [ ] Quantity takeoff QA process
- [ ] Subcontractor coverage minimums per scope (3+ bids)
- [ ] Self-perform vs. sub trade decision rubric

## Pricing strategy

- [ ] Delivery-method margin expectations (lump sum > CMAR > GMP > cost-plus, generally)
- [ ] Contingency by delivery method and design completeness
- [ ] Risk register pricing methodology
- [ ] Escalation clauses (steel, copper, lumber, fuel)
- [ ] Allowances vs. contingencies — discipline
- [ ] Fee structures (fixed fee vs. % vs. shared savings on GMP)

## Risk register pricing

- [ ] Quantitative risk analysis (Monte Carlo) at >$50M projects
- [ ] Top-10 project-risk taxonomy
- [ ] Owner-retained vs. contractor-retained risk

## Decision rubric: Pursuit pricing

- [ ] When to discount fee for strategic account
- [ ] When to walk away on scope-shifted GMP
- [ ] Floor margin by sector (never below X%)
