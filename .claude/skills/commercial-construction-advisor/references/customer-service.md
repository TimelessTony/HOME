# Customer service, quality, warranty

Source authorities: AGC quality programs, Lean Construction Institute, ISO 9001 in construction, Construction Owners Association of America (COAA).

## Punch-list discipline

- [ ] Rolling punch-list during construction (not end-of-job)
- [ ] Pre-final walk vs. final walk vs. post-occupancy
- [ ] Closeout milestone definitions (substantial completion, final completion)
- [ ] O&M / warranty packet standards
- [ ] As-built deliverables

## Warranty programs

- [ ] 11-month warranty walk standard
- [ ] Warranty call routing and SLA
- [ ] Warranty cost reserve (typical 0.25–0.5% of revenue)
- [ ] Sub warranty pass-through tracking

## Quality management

- [ ] Pre-installation meetings per scope
- [ ] First-article / mock-up discipline (curtain wall, drywall partition, finishes)
- [ ] QC checklists by trade
- [ ] NCR (non-conformance report) process

## Customer feedback

- [ ] NPS survey to owner at substantial completion + 12 months
- [ ] Owner relationship reviews quarterly during construction
- [ ] Lessons-learned capture into estimating database

## Incident response

- [ ] Customer complaint escalation tree (Super → PM → PX → COO)
- [ ] Response-time SLA (acknowledge <24h, resolution plan <72h)
- [ ] Root-cause analysis template
- [ ] Claim avoidance: documentation discipline (daily reports, RFIs, photo logs)

## Decision rubric: Claim vs. continue

- [ ] When to formally notice a claim (preserve rights)
- [ ] When to absorb to preserve relationship
- [ ] Owner-relationship value vs. claim value math
