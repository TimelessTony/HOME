# Business development & sales

Source authorities: SMPS Foundation research, FMI BD studies, ENR pursuit-win analyses.

## Pipeline math

- [ ] Coverage ratio target (4–6x of revenue goal)
- [ ] Hit rate by delivery method (negotiated 50–70%, CMAR 25–40%, hard bid 10–20%)
- [ ] Pursuit cost as % of revenue (typical 0.5–1.5%)
- [ ] Pursuit cost as % of contract value (target <2% for negotiated, <0.5% for hard bid)

## Account-based pursuit

- [ ] Top-25 target-owner list discipline
- [ ] Account plan template (relationships, projects, competitors, win themes)
- [ ] Cadence: quarterly executive touches, monthly mid-level, weekly project-level
- [ ] CRM hygiene minimum (HubSpot / Salesforce / Unanet / Cosential / Deltek Vantagepoint)

## Capture planning

- [ ] Capture plan template (decision criteria, decision-makers, win themes, ghost themes, pricing posture, team)
- [ ] Color-team review cadence (Pink, Red, Gold)
- [ ] Win-theme discipline tied to owner's evaluation criteria

## Go/No-Go rubric

- [ ] Strategic-fit score (sector, geography, owner, partner)
- [ ] Win-probability score (incumbent, relationship, qualifications, schedule, fee posture)
- [ ] Risk score (scope clarity, schedule, team, contract terms)
- [ ] Capacity check (PMs, Supers, bonding, working capital)
- [ ] Bid/no-bid threshold and approval level

## Preconstruction as a sales tool

- [ ] Free-precon investment limits
- [ ] Conversion rate from precon-to-construction (target >75%)
- [ ] Owner-paid precon when scope expands
