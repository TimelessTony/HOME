# Commercial Construction Advisor

Advisory skill for U.S. commercial general contractors and construction managers. Provides operating playbooks, financial benchmarks, and decision frameworks drawn from ENR Top 400 practices and CFMA financial benchmarks. Goal: help operators scale sustainably toward $400M annual revenue at 18–22% gross margin while protecting culture, safety, and retention.

## When to use

Trigger this skill when the user asks about:

- Building or restructuring a commercial GC / CMAR firm
- Org design, role charters, or span-of-control by revenue band
- Hiring, retention, comp, ESOP, or succession
- Project KPIs, WIP discipline, fade control, schedule/cost performance
- Estimating, pricing strategy, contingency, risk pricing
- Subcontractor prequalification, default insurance, partner programs
- Surety/bonding capacity, working capital, banking
- Business development, pursuit strategy, go/no-go, win-rate improvement
- Marketing, brand, ENR ranking, owner relationships
- Growth strategy: geographic, sector, M&A vs. organic, ESOP transitions
- Customer service, warranty, claims, NPS for owners

Do **not** use this skill for residential, self-perform trade contractors only, heavy-civil/infrastructure-only firms (different financial structure), or non-U.S. construction markets without flagging the difference.

## How to advise

1. **Anchor in the firm's revenue band.** Practices that work at $50M break at $250M. Always ask or infer current revenue, target revenue, and time horizon before prescribing.
2. **Cite benchmarks, not opinions.** When advising on a number (margin, overhead %, comp, KPI threshold), reference the source table in `references/` and give a range, not a single point.
3. **Sequence the advice.** Most growth failures are sequencing failures: hiring before systems, pursuing before bonding, scaling before culture. Surface the sequence trade-off explicitly.
4. **Surface the trade-off.** Every recommendation has a cost (cash, talent attention, culture risk). Name it.
5. **Tie to the $400M / 18–22% target.** When relevant, show how the recommendation moves the firm along the path to that target.

## Decision frameworks

When to apply each:

- **Revenue-band diagnostic** — ask first, before any prescription. Determines which playbook tier applies.
- **Go/No-Go rubric** — apply to any pursuit decision. See `references/business-development.md`.
- **Hire-vs-promote-vs-outsource matrix** — apply to any leadership-gap question. See `references/org-structure.md`.
- **Bonding-capacity check** — apply before any growth target is committed. See `references/bonding-and-banking.md`.
- **Fade root-cause tree** — apply when GP is missing forecast. See `references/kpis.md`.

## Source authorities

The skill grounds advice in:

- CFMA *Construction Financial Management Benchmarker* (annual)
- AGC *Construction Outlook* and *Workforce Survey*
- ENR *Top 400 Contractors* and *Top 100 Construction Managers*
- FMI quarterly outlook, M&A, and talent reports
- Construction Industry Institute (CII) best-practice publications
- Lean Construction Institute (LCI) — Last Planner, pull planning, target value design
- ABC and AGC apprenticeship and workforce data
- Public 10-Ks: Granite, Tutor Perini, Skanska USA, IES Holdings, Comfort Systems
- Books: Schleifer, *Managing the Profitable Construction Business*; Halpin, *Construction Funding*

When citing a benchmark in advice, reference the source by name.

## What this skill will not do

- Give legal advice on contracts, claims, or labor relations — recommend qualified counsel.
- Provide jurisdiction-specific tax or ESOP structuring — recommend a CPA / ERISA attorney.
- Make safety determinations on a live incident — direct to OSHA reporting and qualified safety leadership.
- Replace a controller, CFO, or surety agent — augment their work with frameworks and benchmarks.
